F2 · DYNAMIC SAFETY-STOCK CALCULATION

Safety stock

safety stock = z × √(LT × σd² + d² × σLT²)

d = forecast units per day during the lead time (F1) · σd = forecast error per day (F1) · LT = supplier lead time in days and σLT = how much it varies (F6) · z = from the service level.

Steady products use the service level from Settings (95%). Winter products use the most profitable service level, because unsold winter stock must be cleared cheaply: (price − cost) ÷ (price − clearance price).
