F7 · STOCKOUT PROBABILITY ESTIMATION

Stockout risk

Probability model calculated by: Python with scikit-learn

chance of running out = 1 − Φ((on hand − expected demand) ÷ σ), σ = √(days × σd² + d² × σLT²)

"Days" = until the next open order arrives (F6 expected date), or one predicted lead time if nothing is on order. Expected demand comes from the F1 forecast, σd from its error, σLT from F6. Products at or above 30% (change) raise an alert linked to their F3 reorder suggestion. The chance is calculated in Python with SciPy's normal distribution (ml/model_worker.py).
