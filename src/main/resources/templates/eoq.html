Stocking for the usual 60 days (change) in a short winter leaves stock unsold, which must then be cleared below cost. The recommendation uses the newsvendor rule: stock = expected demand + z × uncertainty, where z comes from the most profitable chance of not running out, (price − cost) ÷ (price − clearance price). Profits below count every unit held at its purchase cost.
