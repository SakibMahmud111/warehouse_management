Unusual sales weeks

Anomaly check calculated by: Python with scikit-learn

Each of the last 12 weeks is compared with what the F1 model expected for that week. The model already knows about cold days and holidays, so a blanket spike in a cold week is not unusual.

A week is flagged when it is at least 50% (change) above or below the forecast and at least 5 units away. Mark each one as explained (with a note) or under investigation. If the sales are corrected, an open alert closes itself. The comparison runs in Python with NumPy (ml/model_worker.py).
