How the planning features work together

  1. F6 Lead times learns how many days each supplier takes from past deliveries.
  2. F1 Demand forecasting predicts this winter's length from temperatures and learns each product's weekly sales (winter products sell more on cold days; Eid weeks get an uplift).
  3. F2 Safety stock uses the forecast error and supplier lead time to size the buffer.
  4. F4 Order quantity picks the cheapest order size (EOQ) for steady products, and the most profitable winter stock for winter products.
  5. F3 Reorder points = lead-time demand + safety stock, and creates reorder suggestions with the F4 quantity.
  6. F7 Stockout risk estimates the chance of running out before the next delivery and links each alert to its reorder suggestion.
  7. F20 Demand anomalies flags weeks whose sales were far from the forecast.

Everything is recalculated automatically whenever sales, temperatures, products or settings change.
