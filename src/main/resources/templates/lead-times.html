Supplier lead times

Prediction model trained by: Python with scikit-learn

prediction = 0.3 × newest lead time + 0.7 × previous prediction (each new delivery counts 30%, so a supplier that is getting slower is noticed quickly).

Lead time = days from ordering to receiving. With fewer than 3 recorded deliveries the product's manual value is used. The prediction and its spread feed F2 safety stock, the winter order-by date in F4, and F7 stockout risk. Every receipt is compared with what was predicted when the order was placed.
